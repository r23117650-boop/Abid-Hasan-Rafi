SEO Expertise

SEO foundations I focus on

These are key areas of search engine optimization that shape my professional interests and work. Every website needs a strategy tailored to its audience, goals, and current performance.

⌕

Keyword Research

Understand what people search for, identify relevant topics, and map search intent to useful pages and content opportunities.

◎

On-Page SEO

Improve page titles, meta descriptions, headings, internal links, and content structure so pages are clear to users and search engines.

⌘

Technical SEO

Pay attention to crawlability, indexability, canonical URLs, site architecture, mobile usability, and technical issues that can affect discovery.

✎

SEO Content

Plan people-first content around genuine questions, topical relevance, clear explanations, and helpful page experiences.

↗

Off-Page SEO

Learn about reputation, relevant mentions, and sustainable authority-building while avoiding manipulative link schemes.

▥

SEO Measurement

Use performance indicators to evaluate visibility, clicks, engagement, and conversions, then prioritize improvements based on evidence.

My Approach

A practical, user-first SEO process

Strong SEO is ongoing work. A clear process makes it easier to find problems, focus on priorities, and learn from results.

STEP 01

Discover

Understand the business, target audience, search intent, and website goals.

STEP 02

Audit

Review technical health, content quality, site structure, and existing search performance.

STEP 03

Optimize

Prioritize improvements to pages, content, internal links, and technical foundations.

STEP 04

Measure & Learn

Monitor changes, evaluate outcomes, and refine the plan based on real data.
