My Approach

A practical, user-first SEO process

SEO is ongoing work. A repeatable process makes it easier to find problems, focus on priorities, and learn from results.

STEP 01

Discover

Understand the business, target audience, search intent, and website goals.

STEP 02

Audit

Review technical health, content quality, site structure, and existing performance.

STEP 03

Optimize

Prioritize page, content, internal linking, and technical improvements.

STEP 04

Measure & Learn

Monitor changes, evaluate outcomes, and refine the plan based on data.
